# Pluro Fertility and IVF

> Pluro is India's most trusted fertility and IVF platform — a co-creation model built with doctors, for patients. It delivers personalised, evidence-based reproductive care through a growing network of clinics across India, combining advanced clinical science, AI-assisted embryology, standardised treatment protocols, and transparent pricing.

Pluro was founded to bridge the gap in India's fragmented fertility care ecosystem. Most IVF centres historically operated in isolation — without standardised protocols, modern technology, or a patient-first model. Pluro addresses this by uniting leading fertility specialists under a single platform that provides infrastructure, operational support, and technology while preserving each doctor's clinical autonomy. The company has raised ₹125 crore in Series A funding led by Bessemer Venture Partners at a ₹1,000 crore valuation.

## Treatments

> Full detail: https://pluro.in/treatments

Pluro offers a comprehensive range of fertility treatments across five categories:

### Female Fertility

- **Sexual Dysfunction (Female)**: Counselling and treatment for conditions such as vaginismus and unconsummated marriage, including use of serial dilators.
  URL: https://pluro.in/treatments/sexual-dysfunction-female

- **Oral Ovulation Induction (OI)**: Stimulates the ovaries to grow and release eggs. Uses clomiphene citrate or letrozole, with ultrasound monitoring. Recommended for women with PCOS or irregular ovulation.
  URL: https://pluro.in/treatments/oral-ovulation-induction

- **IUI — Intrauterine Insemination**: Washed and concentrated sperm placed directly into the uterus near ovulation. Indicated for mild male infertility, unexplained infertility, mild endometriosis, or PCOS.
  URL: https://pluro.in/treatments/intrauterine-insemination

- **IVF — In Vitro Fertilisation / ICSI**: Advanced fertilisation treatment where eggs are fertilised with sperm in a laboratory. ICSI involves injecting a single sperm directly into each egg. Structured process designed to maximise chances of a healthy pregnancy.
  URL: https://pluro.in/treatments/in-vitro-fertilisation

### Andrology Services (Male Fertility)

Specialised diagnostics and treatments for male infertility — from semen analysis to targeted medical and surgical interventions.

### Preservation & Storage

Safe cryopreservation of eggs, sperm, or embryos for future use — supporting both medical and elective fertility preservation.

### Third Party Reproduction

Carefully guided donor and surrogacy programs for individuals and couples where using their own eggs, sperm, or uterus is not possible. Conducted with full ethical oversight.

### Diagnostic & Surgical Care

Comprehensive fertility diagnostics to assess reproductive health and identify underlying issues — including hormonal tests, ultrasounds, hysteroscopy, laparoscopy, and more.
